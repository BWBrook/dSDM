# Decision

## Categorical support repair

The fixed 77-column schema remains.  Continuous columns 0:25 are always
active.  Each categorical indicator is active only when it occurs in the
complete permitted target replay covariates for that case.  Assessment-only
indicators are mapped to zero, matching Goal028's neutral all-zero policy.

The support mask is never fit from held labels, held covariates, or outer
material.  Applying it must leave discovery, stopping, and replay arrays byte
identical; held changes are recorded only as aggregate diagnostics.

## Revalidation boundary

The original 40 Goal033 bundles are immutable inputs.  Corrected leaves carry
fresh identities including the support-mask digest and cannot share old
leaves.  The workload remains 960 screen plus 480 shortlist logical
evaluations (2,880 physical fits), with three Mac workers and outer `0/0`.
The runner is authorised and execution-ready; this implementation turn did
not launch production fits.  Outer assessment remains unauthorized and
sealed.

## Observed defect

Independent full-endpoint audit reproduces 27/40 affected cases, 66 held rows,
and 55 case-column occurrences.  The broader endpoint audit found 238/280
affected cases and 1,118 held rows.  The published `resmlp13` winner is
therefore provisional until corrected architecture selection is complete.
