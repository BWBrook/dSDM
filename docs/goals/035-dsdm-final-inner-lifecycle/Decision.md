# Decision

## Categorical support repair

The fixed 77-column schema remains.  Continuous columns 0:25 are always
active.  Each categorical indicator is active only when it occurs in the
complete permitted target replay covariates for that case.  Assessment-only
indicators are mapped to zero, matching Goal028's neutral all-zero policy.

The support mask is never fit from held labels, held covariates, or outer
material.  Applying it must leave discovery, stopping, and replay arrays byte
identical; held changes are recorded only as aggregate diagnostics.

## Revalidation boundary

The original 40 Goal033 bundles are immutable inputs.  Corrected leaves carry
fresh identities including the support-mask digest and cannot share old
leaves.  The workload remains 960 screen plus 480 shortlist logical
evaluations (2,880 physical fits), with three Mac workers and outer `0/0`.
The corrected architecture revalidation completed under those fresh
identities and retained `resmlp13`.  Outer assessment remains unauthorized and
sealed.

## Observed defect

Independent full-endpoint audit reproduces 27/40 affected cases, 66 held rows,
and 55 case-column occurrences.  The broader endpoint audit found 238/280
affected cases and 1,118 held rows.  Corrected architecture selection is now
complete and confirms `resmlp13` under the frozen ranking rule.

## Final inner lifecycle

Accept the final inner-lifecycle run as complete.  All 40 target-by-held-fold
cases validated under the corrected architecture, frozen Goal034 runtime, and
one source/config identity.  The private leaves retain five seeds, seven
sampling endpoints, and six method predictions; the tracked aggregate retains
only inventory, provenance, runtime, and arithmetic evidence.

The completed workload contains 8,840 logical artifacts and 17,644 physical
fits.  Thirty-six q-linear fits converged normally.  Four cases reached the
5,000-iteration ceiling and used the prespecified single 10,000-iteration
comparison; all remained finite and their maximum downstream probability
change was `4.3542e-5`, below the `1e-4` stability gate.  This is accepted as
transparent engineering evidence, not represented as formal convergence.

The Apple scikit-learn matmul warning triplets remain a recorded runtime
residual.  They do not block this run because coefficients, logits,
probabilities, all 8,400 retained target prediction slices, and both canary
repeats were finite and internally consistent.  No row-level labels,
coordinates, model weights, or private paths enter tracked evidence, and outer
attempted/opened remains `0/0`.

This decision validates the inner prediction substrate only.  It does not
rank the six methods, establish a dSDM advantage, choose decision thresholds,
or authorise outer assessment; those require a separate prespecified analysis
and freeze decision.
