# Goal 048 - SEAWFB bioregional outer-design freeze

## Scientific objective

Prospectively select and freeze three complete IBRA7.1 bioregions as a
simultaneous outer assessment for the south-east Australian woodland/forest
bird (SEAWFB) case before any model outcome is produced. The three regions must
represent:

1. geographic transfer into an environmentally supported region;
2. transfer to an environmental-edge region; and
3. transfer to a fragmented or land-use-mosaic region.

Tasmania is ordinary training geography and is not a holdout candidate. The
selected regions are fixed challenge environments, not a random or replicated
sample of south-east Australia.

## Question, estimand, and observation process

The later experiment asks whether species-rich dSDM pretraining improves
geographic transfer of relative habitat-suitability discrimination over a
matched target-only neural model and strong conventional SDMs.

The primary estimand is the paired method contrast in ROC AUC within each
estimable target-species x holdout-region task. Evaluation labels distinguish
accepted presence cells from target-group, effort-supported background cells.
They are not true absences, occupancy states, abundance, or independent survey
visits. ALA evidence is opportunistic and uneven in space, time, taxa, observer
effort, and detectability. The atomic response unit is therefore a species x
eligible 0.01-degree cell over 2001-2025, not an individual record.

## Frozen scope

- Verify the official IBRA7.1 authority as 89 bioregions and 419 subregions.
  A holdout is the complete dissolved geometry of one official bioregion.
- Reconcile the existing south-east bird frame to complete IBRA regions before
  any occurrence census. Expand the prospective frame where the frozen rule
  requires it; never call a clipped subregion bundle a whole bioregion.
- Separate three species universes: observation-effort donors, auxiliary
  pretraining heads, and focal evaluation targets. A species may occupy more
  than one universe, but every role and exclusion reason must be explicit.
- Freeze seasonal ecology from literature and ecological sources before
  counts. Effort donors may include regularly recorded native terrestrial
  birds regardless of seasonal use. Auxiliary and target roles may include
  residents and regularly recurring seasonal migrants under the integrated
  2001-2025 habitat-use estimand, but exclude strongly nomadic or irruptive
  taxa whose pooled distribution is not a stable static target. Ambiguous taxa
  stop for scientific review rather than being selected by observed support.
- Use unique accepted species-cell evidence. Record counts, taxon identity,
  prevalence, and expected model performance cannot rank candidate regions.
- Permit aggregate target-by-region counts only as hard estimability gates.
  Candidate role scores use predictors, geography, vegetation, and land use
  only.
- Select all three regions jointly against the same simultaneous retained
  training complement and apply the exact deterministic rule in the contract.
- Exclude each holdout plus a 50 km external buffer from every development
  path. The buffer is a pragmatic guardrail against immediate boundary
  neighbours, not a claim that residual spatial dependence is removed.
- Preserve a strict firewall: no holdout or buffer occurrence can influence
  pretraining, target fitting, transforms or categorical vocabularies,
  pseudoabsence support, internal CV, tuning, stopping, thresholds,
  architecture or family selection, target subsets, or auxiliary-species
  learning-curve decisions.
- Freeze target not recorded plus `X=10` distinct other effort-universe species
  as the primary target-group background rule. Freeze `X=20` as an
  evaluation-only stricter sensitivity. The focal target never contributes to
  its own donor count.
  Richness or effort summaries are prohibited as model predictors.

## Acceptance criteria

Goal 048 is complete only when all of the following hold:

1. The official IBRA source, version, feature counts, region/subregion mapping,
   geometries, licence, retrieval time, and hashes are authenticated.
2. Every candidate and selected holdout has complete official bioregion
   coverage. The prospective grid is expanded response-blind where required,
   or the goal stops if three defensible complete regions cannot be secured.
3. Count-free, reviewed ledgers freeze the effort, auxiliary, and target
   universes before occurrence counts or payloads are inspected.
4. A bounded, disposable minimal-field feasibility census reports only unique
   accepted cells, target-excluded `X=10`/`X=20` support, spatial coverage, and
   aggregate estimability. It is not canonical or bulk bird acquisition.
5. A bounded predictor census proves that one portable predictor ontology can
   cover the prospective domain and supplies response-free environmental and
   fragmentation scores.
6. Before counts or candidate scoring, a tracked ontology maps the
   checksum-bound official NVIS 7 code-label authority to native woody or
   non-woody classes. Ambiguous, unknown, and no-data codes remain missing;
   neither maps nor candidate results may classify them. The exact mapping and
   its digest enter the freeze receipt.
7. The joint deterministic selector returns one feasible, stable,
   nonadjacent, spatially separated triple satisfying the three role
   definitions, the 50 km buffer, and every estimability gate.
8. At least 20 target species form a common primary panel that remains
   estimable in the retained training geography and in all three holdouts.
9. One compact config, one aggregate candidate/decision receipt, and one map
   freeze the domain, species roles, selected triple, scoring, tie-breaks,
   buffer, firewall, estimability, metrics, aggregation, and source hashes.
10. Validation replays the aggregate census from ignored evidence, proves that
   model outcome counters remain zero, and passes one focused independent
   scientific audit.

If the deterministic top triple passes every gate and its assigned roles agree
with the frozen response-free diagnostics, no mandatory Barry pause is
required. Stop and escalate rather than weakening the design if fewer than
three whole-region solutions exist, the ranking is unstable or ambiguous, a
portable predictor has a material coverage/ontology defect, or the selected
triple is scientifically incoherent.

## Later evaluation contract

Internal spatial CV will use retained training geography only. ROC AUC is the
primary outer metric. Balanced accuracy, TSS, MCC, sensitivity, and
specificity are diagnostics using thresholds frozen from training-only OOF
predictions. The primary report gives paired method contrasts for every common
species x region task, then equal-species means within each region and an
equal-region mean across the three regions. Regions, species, cells, folds,
subsets, and seeds are not naive independent replicates. Calibration or
occupancy-probability claims are prohibited.

## Non-goals

This goal does not authorise canonical or bulk bird acquisition, construction
of the final response/predictor tables, model scaffolding, fitting, prediction,
threshold estimation, architecture revision, method rescue, manuscript
revision, or publication claims. Historical bird products are archaeological
checks only.
