privacy-safe evidence for eight downstream focal species. The analytical unit
is one stable camera site by focal species across the whole multi-year source.

presence dominance within the camera source.

its remnant contemporary range is historically constrained.

This goal performs offline evidence preparation only. It stops before ALA-camera
combination, pseudoabsence, predictors, folds, mapping, occupancy or abundance
inference, and modelling.
