# Goal 037 - Frozen Version 1 outer-ready panel

## Objective

Prepare the complete eleven-method Tasmanian mammal panel for a later single,
irreversible outer assessment without opening or scoring the outer vault.

This goal turns the accepted inner work into final models fitted on all
permitted outer-training data. It preserves the six neural paths and all five
conventional families selected within family. It does not tune, rescue, rank,
or remove methods after Goal 036.

## Required result

1. Validate the 952 Goal 029 selected conventional full-training refits at the
   immutable producer and again after a private local copy using every frozen
   challenge prediction.
2. Extract exactly 1,760 five-fold neural epoch policies from the accepted
   Goal 035 leaves and use the sorted lower median for each full-training fit.
3. Export eight private full-training bundles using the frozen outer transform,
   seven nested target endpoints, training-fitted categorical support masks,
   and seven non-target species with pseudoabsences excluded from auxiliary
   pretraining.
4. Fit eight atomic species packages containing 1,768 neural artifacts: five
   pooled and five multi-head auxiliary models plus one q-linear model and 210
   target models per species. Full fits use exact epochs and no validation or
   outer labels.
5. Prove reset, pooled-transfer, multi-head-transfer, common target-head,
   q-initialisation, frozen-trunk, serialization, and challenge-replay gates.
6. Bind the two numerical runtime lanes, all model inventories, Goal 036
   thresholds, scorer source, privacy boundary, and authorities into one
   immutable aggregate transaction.
7. Keep outer attempted/opened exactly `0/0`, commit the accepted package, and
   push `main`.

## Non-goals

- opening, predicting, scoring, thresholding, or mapping the outer assessment;
- changing `resmlp13`, the six neural paths, the five conventional families,
  seeds, endpoints, thresholds, predictors, or primary contrasts;
- larger-network, dropout, regularisation, reduced-predictor, mainland-range,
  geographic-transfer, or bird experiments;
- ecological claims from full-training fits.
