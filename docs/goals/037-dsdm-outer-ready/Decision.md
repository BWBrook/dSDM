# Decision

## Prespecified boundary

Goal 037 prepares the frozen Version 1 panel; it does not assess it. All eleven
Goal 036 methods remain in scope. The six neural paths use `resmlp13`, seeds
31031-31035, and all seven sampling endpoints. The five conventional families
remain intercept, elastic net, random forest, histogram gradient boosting, and
the shallow neural network.

Each neural full-training epoch count is the sorted lower median of the five
matching Goal 035 selected epochs for identical target, endpoint, method, seed,
and architecture provenance. Auxiliary pooled and multi-head fits use the same
rule across their five held-fold fits. Final fits have no early stopping and no
validation labels.

Target support masks are fitted on each complete permitted target endpoint.
The auxiliary mask is fitted on the complete seven-species, target-excluded,
pseudoabsence-free auxiliary view. The fixed 77-column schema remains, with
unsupported categorical indicators mapped to neutral zero. The future scorer
accepts already encoded outer-transform arrays but has no capability to open
the outer vault.

The conventional and neural artifacts retain separate locked numerical
runtimes. A common transaction binds their semantic challenge replays and the
frozen Goal 036 threshold table; it does not force unsafe cross-runtime model
loading.

## Interpretation

Successful completion means the Version 1 experiment is technically ready for
a later one-shot spatial outer assessment. It says nothing about ecological
performance, dSDM superiority, sensitivity outcomes, maps, occupancy
probability, or transfer to birds. Outer attempted/opened must remain `0/0`.

## Deferred sensitivity

Barry's larger/deeper network, dropout/regularisation, and reduced-predictor
ideas are scientifically justified but remain a separately prespecified,
bounded exploratory tranche. Full-range mainland pretraining and a
mainland-to-Tasmania transfer test are distinct geographic experiments. None
may adapt Goal 037 after outer results because Goal 037 does not open them.
