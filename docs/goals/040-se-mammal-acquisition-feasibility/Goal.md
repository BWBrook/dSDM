# Goal 040 — southeastern mammal acquisition and feasibility gate

## Scientific objective

Determine whether real 2001–2025 evidence can support a defensible broad
southeastern-Australian native mammal cohort, a target-group observation-support
substrate, and a genuinely common mainland-plus-Tasmania predictor panel. The
decision authorises or refuses a later mainland-to-Tasmania dSDM experiment; it
does not fit a model or assert that a binary response stack is pretraining-ready.

The observation process is heterogeneous. ALA records are opportunistic,
presence-only, spatially and taxonomically uneven, and may contain repeated
records of the same observation. Camera survey-supported absences are
Tasmania-only designed-survey evidence with distinct provenance. The atomic
normalised evidence unit is a species × eligible 0.01-degree land cell, not an
animal, population, independent visit, or abundance observation. Inference is
restricted to the frozen frame and integrated 2001–2025 period.

## Acceptance

1. Freeze a reviewed taxon ledger before count inspection, with explicit
   inclusion/exclusion reasons, source citations, accepted ALA and AFD
   identities, reviewed descendants/synonyms, and query roles. The final ledger
   is tracked canonical provenance; raw discovery responses stay ignored.
2. Freeze a versioned authoritative Australian land/state boundary manifest
   before count inspection and derive deterministic centroid-in-polygon land
   eligibility and Tasmania/mainland classification.
3. Census and, within the declared resource ceiling, fully acquire immutable
   2001–2025 queries; apply the inherited coordinate, uncertainty, provider-ID,
   deduplication, land-cell, and species-cell QA.
4. Retain threshold-neutral target-group donor counts and nearest-target
   distances; report nested X=2, X=3, and X=5 diagnostics without selecting X
   from performance or camera concordance.
5. Produce a predictor source/definition matrix and response-neutral extraction
   pilot proving one common product and ontology across both regions for every
   retained layer.
6. Publish only aggregate, privacy-safe evidence and a final `ready`, `repair`,
   or `blocked` receipt under the criteria in `Decision.md` and the machine
   contract.

No live count, download, response-payload inspection, model fit, map, outer
access, or manuscript claim is authorised by this prespecification packet.
