# Goal 049 decision record

## D049-001 - two-stage firewall

Species discovery, ecology, taxonomy, query identity, and panel rules are
frozen before expanded occurrence support. Stage B is mechanically blocked
until Stage A passes.

## D049-002 - source-based expanded universe

BirdLife Working List v4.3 supplies the current Australian nomenclatural
crosswalk. Australian Bird Data v1 supplies versioned jurisdiction, native and
extant status, habitat, and movement traits. ALA accepted concepts are the
operational occurrence taxonomy.

## D049-003 - role semantics

Effort donors are broader than modelled taxa. Auxiliary heads and focal target
candidates use non-trivial forest/woodland feeding habitat, exclude nomadic or
irruptive taxa, and exclude prespecified scale/detection-mismatch families.
Regular seasonal migrants stay eligible. Target candidacy deliberately has no
extra subjective specialist filter.

## D049-004 - primary and secondary panels

The all-three-region common panel remains primary. Region-specific panels are
prespecified secondary evidence. This amendment follows the Goal 048 seed
feasibility census but precedes expanded acquisition and every model outcome.
A two-of-three panel is not added.

## D049-005 - immutable Goal 048 reuse

Goal 048 receipts may be promoted only by exact contract identity. Any drift
requires reacquisition under Goal 049; Goal 048 files and receipts remain
unchanged.

## D049-006 - authority crosswalk and ALA unit

The legacy Australian Bird Data identifier is candidate evidence, not an
unconditional join key. A current BirdLife match must have reciprocal name
evidence, frozen Goal 048 evidence, or a reviewed same-family nomenclatural
transition; otherwise a unique exact-name fallback or a count-free exception
is required. Four source taxa are explicitly collapsed to their current ALA
species parent, while *Malurus lamberti* and *M. assimilis* remain distinct
current species and inherit the old broad source traits transparently.

## D049-007 - frozen panel outcome

The source-based universe contains 505 active effort donors and 259 auxiliary
head/focal candidates. Under the prespecified support gates, 73 species enter
the strict common primary panel. The NCP, SEH, and NNC secondary panels contain
88, 122, and 134 species respectively. These are eligibility outcomes, not
model-performance results; they do not alter the frozen regions or gates.

## D049-008 - response and background interpretation

The private substrate records 5,048,061 unique species-cell memberships.
Presence is an accepted occurrence in a domain cell; the contrasting class is
focal-excluded, effort-supported target-group background at `X=10`, with
`X=20` retained only as an evaluation sensitivity. Neither class is an
occupancy estimate or a true absence, and effort richness is not a predictor.
