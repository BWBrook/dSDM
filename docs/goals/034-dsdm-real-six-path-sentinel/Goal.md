# Goal 034 - Real six-path sentinel and compute-route freeze

## Objective

Prove that the frozen `resmlp13` architecture and all six dSDM paths execute
end to end on the real Tasmanian mammal substrate, then select the practical
route for the later full lifecycle. This is an engineering and inferential
boundary gate, not an ecological comparison.

## Acceptance

1. Export one content-addressed numeric bundle for `sarcophilus_harrisii`,
   held `fold01`, stopping `fold02`, seed `31031`, and the authoritative
   ladder00 25%, 50%, and full memberships.
2. Exclude the target, pseudoabsences, unknowns, and held fold from the seven
   auxiliary species. Neural auxiliary loss is the mean of seven within-
   species means; q-linear total sample weight is equal across species even
   when their row counts differ.
3. Execute exactly three reusable auxiliary artifacts and 18 fresh target
   artifacts: 21 logical artifacts and 41 discovery/replay or q fits.
4. Verify reset/replay, nested subsets, matched target heads and q
   initializations, identical frozen/full transfer starts, byte-stable frozen
   trunks, finite outputs, content binding, privacy, and outer access `0/0`.
5. Repeat each candidate route on the same runtime and require identical model
   and prediction digests within route. CPU/CUDA digest equality is not
   required.
6. Compare the production-shaped multihead workload on Mac CPU and Alien CUDA,
   freeze one route, and record the later full-run handoff without launching
   it.
7. Bind every execution source by path and SHA-256. Require the shared Python
   and numerical-library runtime exactly, fail q-linear on nonconvergence, and
   require both repeat receipts to pass the same complete validator.

## Non-goals

- No AUC, TSS, MCC, thresholds, ranking, or ecological interpretation.
- No sealed outer assessment.
- No architecture search or hyperparameter change.
- No 8,400-target plus 440-auxiliary full lifecycle.
- No resumable queue, worker database, or production orchestration framework.
