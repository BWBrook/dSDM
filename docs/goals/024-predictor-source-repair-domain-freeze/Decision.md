# Decision

`repair_not_authorized` is accepted. No source substitution or generic filling
is authorised, and zero predictor values are repaired. The supported
structural domain `D_struct` is frozen as the canonical 28-predictor
nonmissing intersection. Goal023 pooled/species support diagnostics remain
descriptive prepartition evidence and are not structural filters.

`D_struct` is the deliberate primary policy choice: it is high-coverage,
provenance-safe, and retains all 28 predictors; it is not the uniquely
data-determined consequence of the source audit. Any future full-domain,
fold-fitted missing-treatment analysis is sensitivity only, and excluded
cells/rows remain audit and sensitivity material.

The operational estimand is a species-specific relative response score over
canonical cells in `D_struct`, conditional on the accepted
presence/survey-supported-absence/pseudoabsence construction. It is not
detection-corrected occupancy, abundance, population prevalence, or inference
outside `D_struct`.

Post-domain pseudoabsence replay is validated: 587 original rows yield 498
retained and 89 excluded rows, with no retained support-count, distinct-support,
5-km-distance, same-cell support-link, or domain-membership failures. No quota,
balancing, or thinning contract exists; rederivation is therefore unnecessary.
Per-species and fold-level adequacy remains deferred to Goal025.

Inference from the resulting substrate is conditional on `D_struct`; later
goals must recompute categorical novelty and continuous support per species and
training fold. A future authoritative source package opens a new versioned
goal. No Barry decisions are pending for Goal024.
