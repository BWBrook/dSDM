# Bounded novelty check (18 October 2026)

**Question.** Does a close precedent combine target-excluded auxiliary-species pretraining, a matched same-architecture target-only control, and whole-region geographic exclusion for transfer evaluation in species distribution modelling?

**Finding.** No exact predecessor was identified among the six closest verified precedents below. This is evidence for a calibrated statement about the combination of design features, not evidence for an unqualified claim of priority.

| Reference | Closest contribution | Design boundary relative to this study |
|---|---|---|
| Chen et al. (2017), [doi:10.24963/ijcai.2017/509](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2017/509) | Deep multispecies embedding with single-species and multispecies comparisons | Five-fold cross-validation in BCR13; no staged target-excluded auxiliary pretraining or whole-region transfer test identified |
| Brun et al. (2024), [doi:10.1038/s41467-024-48559-9](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-48559-9) | Large multispecies deep-learning community model evaluated with independent expert surveys | No matched target-only network, staged target-excluded pretraining, or held-region transfer design identified |
| Bourel et al. (2025), [doi:10.1111/ele.70177](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ele.70177) | Presence-only representation learning transferred to abundance SDMs, with and without transfer compared | Source and target differ by response/data type; spatial-block cross-validation is not a target-excluded auxiliary-species pretraining experiment with a closed geographic holdout |
| Ovaskainen et al. (2025), [doi:10.1038/s41592-025-02823-y](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02823-y) | Hierarchical transfer from common to rare species, compared with single-species models | Randomized two-fold splitting over 53 sites; hierarchical prior transfer rather than a neural encoder tested under geographic transfer |
| Lange et al. (2025), [PMLR 267](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/lange25a.html) | Few-shot range estimation for evaluation species excluded from multispecies training | Transfer to unseen species, without a matched target-only control or whole-region geographic exclusion |
| Santos et al. (2026), [doi:10.3389/fevo.2026.1904087](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/ecology-and-evolution/articles/10.3389/fevo.2026.1904087/full) | Sequential multispecies learning with fine-tuning and experience replay | Published 14 September 2026; random 80/20 calibration and augmented target data, without whole-region exclusion or a target-excluded auxiliary stage |

The search was bounded rather than systematic. Metadata came from the linked publisher or proceedings records; design boundaries were checked against the corresponding article or proceedings text. The defensible manuscript claim is therefore that the *combination* of controls and geographic evaluation appears uncommon or was not found in this search.
